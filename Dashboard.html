How Smart Irrigation Works on Your Farm

1. Underground Sensors

Wireless sensors buried in the root zone constantly measure how much moisture is left in the soil. When soil gets dry, the sensor automatically sends a signal to open the water valves.

2. Automated Scheduling

Instead of guessing when to water, the system sets exact timers based on local weather forecasts. If heavy rain is coming, scheduled watering pauses automatically to save water.

3. Power & Cost Savings

Powered mostly by solar energy, the automated pump reduces electricity bills and manual labor. Delivering water straight to roots also stops weed growth and prevents root rot.

Simple Steps to Improve Your Soil Health

1. Add Cow Dung & Compost Mix decomposed organic manure before planting season to feed friendly earthworms and retain soil moisture during summer.
2. Practice Crop Rotation Alternate grain crops with legume crops (like moong or soybeans) to naturally put free nitrogen back into the earth.
3. Avoid Over-Tilling Minimizing heavy tractor ploughing protects underground fungal networks and stops topsoil from blowing away in heavy winds.

Expert Recommendations to Boost Your Final Yield

1. Timely Micronutrient Spray Applying zinc and boron sprays during flowering stage prevents flower drop and increases grain/fruit weight by up to 10%.
2. Maintain Soil Moisture Avoid water stress during grain-filling periods. Using automated drip lines prevents root shock and maintains steady growth.
3. Early Pest Scouting Monitor for stem borers or aphids weekly. Catching pest outbreaks early protects total output from unexpected harvest loss.
